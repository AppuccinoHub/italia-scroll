# Video sources (Italia Scroll)

Short muted loops for classroom Chromebooks. Trimmed/compressed locally (~3–5s, ~540p).

All from **Mixkit** (Mixkit License — free for educational use):

- `gelato-girl.mp4` — Happy girl eating ice cream (#41215)
- `rome-photos.mp4` — Tourist girls taking photos in Rome (#9062)
- `rome-streets.mp4` — Sunny Rome streets (#9025)
- `rome-view.mp4` — Rome street view (#11200)
- `street-dance.mp4` — Young people dancing in the street (#51295)
- `beach-run.mp4` — Woman running happily on the beach (#1015)
- `scooter-ride.mp4` — Couple riding scooter (#12975)
- `seaside-run.mp4` — Running toward seaside sunset (#37021)
- `venice-boats.mp4` — Boats in Venice canals (#11003)
- `venice-walk.mp4` — Walking a Venice bridge (#27805)

Action teen stills in `images/hooks/teen-*.jpg` are frames extracted from these clips.
